POPULAR SCHEMES FOR WOMEN

Schemes That Can Help You Start

Three widely used central government schemes for women who want to start or expand a business.

Stand-Up India

BANK LOANS

Bank loans for women entrepreneurs to set up a new business in manufacturing, services or trading.

  • For first-time (new) enterprises
  • Applied through bank branches
Visit Official Portal

PM Mudra Yojana

COLLATERAL-FREE

Collateral-free loans for small and micro enterprises, available in stages based on your business needs.

  • Shishu, Kishore and Tarun categories
  • For new and existing small businesses
Visit Official Portal

PMEGP

CREDIT-LINKED SUBSIDY

Credit-linked subsidy support for setting up new micro enterprises and creating self-employment opportunities.

  • Higher subsidy rate for women applicants
  • Applied online through the PMEGP portal
Visit Official Portal

Loan limits, subsidy rates and rules can change. Always confirm the latest details on the official portal or at your bank branch.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Eligibility & Required Documents

A quick checklist of what is usually needed. Requirements differ by scheme and bank.

Basic Eligibility

  • Indian citizen aged 18 years or above
  • A clear plan for a new or small business
  • No default with any bank or financial institution
  • Business in manufacturing, services or trading

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar card and PAN card
  • Address proof and passport-size photographs
  • Bank account details
  • Business plan or project report
