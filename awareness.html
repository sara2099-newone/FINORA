BUILD YOUR KNOWLEDGE

Understand the Basics

A few essential financial concepts everyone should know.

Saving & Investing

Saving helps you keep money for future needs, while investing aims to grow your money over time. Understand the risks before choosing an investment.

Banking Knowledge

Learn how savings accounts, deposits, digital banking and banking services help you manage your money securely.

Understanding Loans

A loan is borrowed money that must be repaid with applicable interest. Always understand repayment terms before borrowing.

Smart Money Habits

Track your expenses, avoid unnecessary debt, compare financial products and maintain savings for unexpected situations.

PROTECT YOUR MONEY

Stay Financially Safe

Learn how to recognize financial threats and protect your personal information.

Phishing Messages

Avoid suspicious messages and links requesting your personal or banking details.

UPI Payment Safety

Never share your UPI PIN or OTP. You do not need to enter a PIN to receive money.

Protect Your Accounts

Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication and keep your credentials private.

Remember: Stay Alert!

Never share your OTP, PIN, password or confidential banking information with anyone. If you suspect financial fraud in India, report it immediately through the official cybercrime reporting portal or helpline 1930.
